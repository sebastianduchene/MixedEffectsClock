# Random effect and slowdown: what we expect before looking

The first simulation in this project with a genuine random effect and a genuine rate
decrease. Both existing simulations have a true dispersion of essentially zero and no
clade that is really slower than background, so neither tests the half of the model the
log-scale parameterisation exists for.

Generated by `../../sim_re_slowdown/simulate_re_slowdown.R` on the same 50-taxon tree as
the earlier build, under Jukes-Cantor, 10,000 sites.

    log r_i = log(0.005) + log(2)*fast_i + log(0.3)*slow_i + eps_i,   eps_i ~ N(0, 0.3^2)

## Known answers

| Parameter | Truth | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `clockRate` | 0.005 | the background rate |
| `coefficient.1` | +0.6931 | log 2, the fast clade and its sister's stem, 4 branches |
| `coefficient.2` | -1.2040 | log 0.3, the SLOW clade with its stem, 19 branches |
| `ucldStdev` | 0.2816 | realised sd of log eps; nominal was 0.300 |

The realised dispersion, not the nominal 0.3, is the target: 98 draws from a lognormal do
not have exactly the nominal spread, and 0.2816 is what this dataset actually contains.

**The second coefficient is the new thing.** Nothing in this project has yet estimated a
rate decrease from data. If its interval sits at or above zero, the log-scale form is not
delivering what it exists for.

**The dispersion is the other new thing.** Previously its truth was zero and the test was
whether the posterior piled against the boundary. Here it is genuinely non-zero, so this
is a real coverage test for the first time, and it is the parameter that mixes worst.

## Design, checkable exactly

| Column | Branches |
| --- | --- |
| fast | 4 |
| slow | 19 |
| background | 75 |

98 branches for 50 taxa. No branch should be in two columns; the two sets are disjoint by
construction and all three clades are constrained monophyletic.

## Signal available

| Column | Expected substitutions |
| --- | --- |
| background | 1844 |
| fast | 328 |
| slow | 144 |

2091 of the 10,000 sites are variable. The slow clade carries the least signal, so expect
its interval to be the widest of the three in relative terms; that is information, not
failure.

## Expected realised statistics

Mean branch rate should come out near 0.005 and the realised coefficient of variation of
branch rates well above the dispersion, because the fixed effects contribute most of the
spread. If `ucldStdev` comes back near the realised CV, the fixed effects are not doing
their job.

## Substitution model, deliberately mis-specified as before

Jukes-Cantor data, HKY plus gamma fitted, exactly as in the earlier builds. kappa should
sit near 1 and the gamma shape should be poorly determined.
